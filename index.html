The basics before you pull up.

How do I book a vendor table?

Choose your show and tables, pay through PayPal, Cash App or Venmo, select the payment method you used, and submit the booking. The Bullpen will confirm payment and availability.

What can vendors sell?

Sports cards, Pokémon, TCG, slabs and other collectibles. If you're unsure, contact us first.

Is the Halloween show family-friendly?

Yes. The October 31 show is being built as a family-friendly Halloween event, including kids in costume and planned candy/pack giveaways.

How can I hear about new shows?

Follow The Bullpen on your social pages and check this site for newly announced events.
